Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 2026

Who we are

SCConnect Limited ("we", "us", "our") is a supply chain and commercial advisory consultancy incorporated in New Zealand (company number 7414336). We operate the website at scconnect.co.nz.

If you have any questions about this policy, contact us at: Vaughan.Grant@scconnect.co.nz

What information we collect

We collect personal information only when you choose to provide it through our consultation request form. This includes:

  • — Full name and organisation
  • — Email address and phone number
  • — The content of your enquiry message

We do not use cookies, analytics trackers, or any other passive data collection tools on this website.

How we use your information

Information submitted through the contact form is used solely to:

  • — Respond to your enquiry
  • — Schedule and conduct a consultation if relevant

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties for marketing purposes. We do not add you to any mailing list without your explicit consent.

Third-party processors

Your data passes through the following third-party services:

Formspree

Form submissions are processed by Formspree, Inc. (USA). Formspree receives and forwards your submission to us by email. Their privacy policy is available at formspree.io/legal/privacy-policy.

Microsoft 365

We receive and store your enquiry via Microsoft 365 (Outlook). Microsoft's privacy policy is available at privacy.microsoft.com.

GitHub Pages

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages (Microsoft). GitHub may process connection-level data such as IP addresses as part of serving the site. Their privacy statement is available at docs.github.com.

Google reCAPTCHA

Our contact form uses Google reCAPTCHA to prevent automated spam submissions. reCAPTCHA collects hardware and software information and sends it to Google for analysis. Google's privacy policy is available at policies.google.com/privacy.

Data retention

We retain enquiry information only for as long as is necessary to respond to and complete any resulting engagement. If no engagement proceeds, information is deleted within 12 months of receipt.

Your rights under the NZ Privacy Act 2020

Under the New Zealand Privacy Act 2020 you have the right to:

  • — Request access to personal information we hold about you
  • — Request correction of that information if it is inaccurate
  • — Request deletion of your information
  • — Lodge a complaint with the Office of the Privacy Commissioner at privacy.org.nz

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at Vaughan.Grant@scconnect.co.nz. We will respond within 20 working days as required by the Act.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top of this page reflects when it was last revised. Continued use of the website after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
